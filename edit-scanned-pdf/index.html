Edit scanned PDFs with OCR

Scans are just pictures of text. Edivora reads them with OCR on your device, then lets you edit each word in place, keeping the look of the paper and the original lettering.

Open a scanned PDF

Free · no sign-up · no watermark · files stay on your device

How to edit a scanned PDF

  1. Open the scanned PDF or a photo of the document.
  2. Press “Make text editable” under the page (choose extra languages under Options).
  3. Click any recognised text to change it.
  4. Download the PDF, which is also searchable now.

Features

  • OCR runs on your device (Tesseract)
  • Each label and value is its own editable piece, so forms keep their layout
  • Font style, size and ink colour are matched
  • Original letters are removed and the paper texture filled back in
  • English offline; Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, French, German, Spanish and more
  • Unedited text is kept as invisible searchable text

Questions

Is the OCR free and private?

Yes. Recognition runs in your browser; the scan is not uploaded.

Which scans work best?

Straight, clear scans at 200–300 dpi. Handwriting and stamps are often misread, but you can simply retype any piece.
