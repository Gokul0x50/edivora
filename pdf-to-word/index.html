PDF to Word converter

Turn a PDF into a Word document you can edit, with its text, fonts, tables and pictures rebuilt as real Word content. Or go the other way and save a Word file as PDF.

Choose a PDF

Free · no sign-up · no watermark · files stay on your device

How to convert PDF to Word

  1. Open your PDF in Edivora.
  2. Optionally fix things in the PDF first (scanned pages can be made editable with OCR).
  3. Click Download → Word document, or More → Edit as a Word document to keep working on it.
  4. Open the .docx in Microsoft Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice.

Features

  • Text with its fonts, sizes, colours, bold and italic
  • Tables rebuilt as Word tables
  • Pictures kept
  • Scanned PDFs converted after OCR
  • Word → PDF in the same place
  • Runs offline in the browser

Questions

Is the PDF to Word conversion free?

Yes, with no page limit, sign-up or watermark.

Will the Word file look exactly like the PDF?

Text, tables and pictures are rebuilt so they can be edited. Complex layouts with several columns or text over pictures come out as a single column.

Can I convert Word to PDF too?

Yes. Open a .docx file and choose Download → PDF.
