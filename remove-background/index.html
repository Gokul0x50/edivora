Remove an image background

Cut a product or portrait out of a plain or evenly coloured background with one click, tidy the edges with the magic eraser, and save a transparent PNG.

Open a picture

Free · no sign-up · no watermark · files stay on your device

How to remove the background from an image

  1. Open your picture in the image editor.
  2. Click Remove background (in the Edit panel or the More menu).
  3. Use the magic eraser or eraser for any leftovers.
  4. Download as PNG to keep the transparency.

Features

  • One-click removal for plain backgrounds
  • Magic eraser: click a colour to remove it (Shift-click for every match)
  • Eraser with soft edges
  • Place the cut-out on a new background or colour
  • Transparent PNG or WebP export

Questions

Does it work on busy backgrounds?

It works best on plain or evenly coloured backgrounds such as a wall or studio backdrop. For busy scenes use the magic eraser and eraser.

Is it free?

Yes, with full-resolution downloads and no watermark.
