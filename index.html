Questions

Is Edivora free?

Yes. Every editor is free to use with no sign-up, no watermark and no limits on the number of files. The source code is open on GitHub.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Edivora runs entirely in your browser. PDFs, Word files, photos, audio and video are opened, edited and saved on your own device, so they stay private.

Can I edit the existing text in a PDF?

Yes. Click any paragraph to change it in a matching font and size; text below moves down like in a word processor. Tables, images and form fields can be edited too, and scanned PDFs can be made editable with OCR.

Can I convert PDF to Word?

Yes. Download any PDF as an editable Word (.docx) file, or open it in the Word editor first. Word documents can also be saved as PDF.

Which video formats can I export?

MP4 (H.264) or WebM video, animated GIF, MP3 or WAV sound, and single frames as PNG. Fast export uses the browser's built-in encoder in Chrome and Edge.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Every editor works on phones and tablets, with touch gestures, a mobile layout and pinch to zoom.
