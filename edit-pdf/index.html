How to edit a PDF online

  1. Open Edivora and choose your PDF (or drop it on the page).
  2. Click any paragraph, table cell or image to change it, or pick a tool to add text, a signature, shapes or highlights.
  3. Use Undo, the Original / Edited switch and Find & replace while you work.
  4. Download the edited PDF. Your file never leaves your device.

Features

  • Edit existing text with matched fonts, sizes, colours, bold and italic
  • Tables grow with their text; add or remove rows and columns

  • Highlight, whiteout and true redaction

Questions

Can I edit the existing text of a PDF for free?

Yes. Click a paragraph and type. Edivora matches the original font as closely as possible and reflows the content below it. There are no limits and no watermark.

Is it safe to edit a confidential PDF here?

The PDF is opened and saved inside your browser and is never sent to a server. Use Redact (not Whiteout) to remove sensitive content permanently.

Can I edit a scanned PDF?

Yes. Scanned pages show a “Make text editable” button that runs OCR on your device.
