How to convert a video to GIF

  1. Open the video in the video editor.
  2. Trim it to the part you want (split and delete the rest).
  3. Add text if you like.
  4. Choose Download → Animated GIF and pick the width and frame rate.

Features

  • Trim and split precisely
  • Add captions and stickers
  • 320–800 px wide, 8–20 fps
  • Also export MP4, WebM or a still PNG

Questions

How long can the GIF be?

Any length, but GIFs grow large quickly; a few seconds at 480 px is ideal for chats.
