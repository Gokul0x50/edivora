How to edit a video online

  1. Open your videos, pictures and music.
  2. Trim, split and arrange them on the timeline; change speed and volume.
  3. Add text, captions, transitions, motion and colour filters.
  4. Export MP4 or WebM at up to 4K, or a GIF.

Features

  • Multitrack timeline with overlays and audio tracks
  • Trim, split, duplicate, speed 0.25×–4×, freeze frame
  • Titles, captions (.srt import/export), shapes, backgrounds
  • Fade, slide, zoom, wipe, blur and spin transitions; Ken Burns motion
  • Filters and colour adjustments
  • 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 sizes; MP4, WebM, GIF, MP3 export

Questions

Is there a watermark?

No. Exports are clean and free.

Which browser is fastest?

Chrome and Edge export fastest with the built-in encoder. Other browsers record the video in real time.
