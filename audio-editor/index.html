How to edit audio online

  1. Open MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG or FLAC files (or the sound of a video), or record from your microphone.
  2. Drag across the waveform to select, then cut, delete, silence or keep only that part.
  3. Apply effects such as noise reduction, voice clean-up, EQ, tempo or pitch.
  4. Download as MP3 or WAV.

Features

  • Multitrack timeline with volume, pan, mute and solo
  • Record over other tracks
  • Noise reduction, voice clean-up, equalizer, compressor
  • Speed, tempo (keeps pitch), pitch (keeps length)
  • Echo, reverb, vocal remover, remove silence, fades, normalize
  • MP3 128–320 kbps, WAV 16/24/32-bit

Questions

Can I extract the audio from a video?

Yes. Open the video file in the audio editor and download the sound as MP3 or WAV.

Is my recording uploaded?

No. Recording and editing happen in your browser.
