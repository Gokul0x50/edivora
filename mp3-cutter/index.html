How to cut an MP3

  1. Open the MP3 (or WAV, M4A, video…).
  2. Drag across the part you want to keep and press “Keep only this”.
  3. Add a fade in and fade out to the clip.
  4. Download as MP3.

Features

  • Precise selection with start, end and length fields
  • Keep only the selection or delete parts
  • Fade in / fade out handles
  • Join several songs on one track
  • Volume and normalize
  • MP3 or WAV

Questions

How do I make a ringtone?

Select about 30 seconds, press Keep only this, add short fades and download as MP3.
